OUR PROCESS — 5 PHASES · 4—8 WEEKS PER PROJECT

From idea to
final frame.

We move at the pace a story deserves. Most
projects close in four to eight weeks; some take
longer because they should.

Start a project

  1. 01

    Discovery

    A long conversation. Mood, brief, references, expectations.

    1 WEEK

  2. 02

    Mood Board

    A visual contract — references, palette, casting direction.

    3—5 DAYS

  3. 03

    Pre-production

    Scout, casting, schedule, crew. The boring miracle of logistics.

    1—2 WEEKS

  4. 04

    Shoot Day

    On set. Quiet, intentional, generous. We aim for honest frames.

    1—3 DAYS

  5. 05

    Post & Deliver

    Edit, retouch, master files, prints. Hand-finished.

    2—3 WEEKS
